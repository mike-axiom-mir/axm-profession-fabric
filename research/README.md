# AXM Profession Research Intake

Profession Research sits **upstream** of the Profession Compiler.

Its job is to gather external evidence about human work without silently turning that evidence into Professional Body canon.

Pipeline:

`source evidence -> research packet -> review/contradictions -> compiler proposal -> profession package -> professional evidence`

## Rules

1. A source is evidence about some layer of work, not "the profession truth".
2. Preserve source version, concept type, collection/authoring method, licensing mode, and crosswalk uncertainty.
3. Crosswalks may be exact, broader, narrower, partial, or adjacent. Never force one occupation/work-role code onto an AXM profession.
4. Restricted/reference-only sources may be cited as metadata but their protected content is not ingested into the compiler.
5. Research packets do not modify Professional Bodies or maturity records automatically.
6. Contradictions and gaps remain visible until a later compiler/review step resolves them.
7. Local/versioned downloadable data is preferred for large open datasets so future runs are reproducible and not dependent on a live API.
8. High-stakes professions require explicit authorization/safety non-claims even at research time.

## Current pilot

The first intake pilot compares public occupational/domain evidence against five existing bodies:

- Software QA / Playtest
- UX Researcher
- Application Security Engineer
- Statistician
- Maintenance Engineer

These packets are **gap analyses**, not automatic repairs. They exist to test the intake before Profession Fabric scales the remaining target map.
