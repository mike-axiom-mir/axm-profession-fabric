# Profession Research Decisions

Append/supersede; do not silently rewrite prior research decisions.

## 2026-09-12 — Research stays upstream of professional canon

**Decision:** External occupational/domain research produces versioned Research Packets. It does not directly rewrite Professional Bodies, Profession Package maturity, or institutional state.

**Consequence:** A later explicit Profession Compiler/review delta must decide whether a research finding becomes package knowledge, a tool/procedure, a fixture, a handoff, a boundary, or remains only contextual evidence.

## 2026-09-12 — Source truth types remain distinct

**Decision:** Occupational tasks, expert taxonomy relations, employer-posting tool evidence, workforce Work Roles, formal requirements, public context profiles, and observed AXM project behavior remain separately labelled evidence types.

**Consequence:** The compiler must not flatten heterogeneous evidence into an unlabeled list of skills or treat frequency, relevance, requirement, and observed behavior as equivalent claims.

## 2026-09-12 — Profession crosswalks are many-to-many by default

**Decision:** An AXM profession is not assumed to equal one O*NET, ESCO, ISCO, NICE, or other external concept.

**Consequence:** Every mapping declares concept type plus `exact`, `broader`, `narrower`, `partial`, or `adjacent` relation and confidence. Multiple mappings and explicit non-mappings are normal.

## 2026-09-12 — Rights constrain ingestion independently of technical access

**Decision:** Machine-readable or publicly viewable content is not automatically ingestible. Source-specific reuse terms are part of the research contract.

**Consequence:** O*NET and ESCO may be locally ingested/adapted under their stated reuse conditions. Sources such as SFIA, ISO standards text, and IEEE standards text remain reference-only under current rights. A future permission/license may explicitly supersede a source's legal mode.

## 2026-09-12 — First five research pilots are gap audits, not repairs

**Decision:** Software QA / Playtest, UX Researcher, Application Security Engineer, Statistician, and Maintenance Engineer are the first Body-reviewed Research Packets.

**Consequence:** Their findings test source policy, crosswalk semantics, snapshot continuity, and gap detection. No Professional Body or scoped TEST maturity record is changed by this research pass.
