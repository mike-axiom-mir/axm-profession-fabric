# AI / Data backbone cohort

Five isolated EXPERIMENTAL Professional Packages: Machine Learning Engineer, AI Evaluation Engineer, AI Systems Engineer, Knowledge Engineer, and Data Engineer.

This cohort exists to make intelligence + Professional Body combinations measurable and operable without collapsing model capability, profession capability, system integration, knowledge governance, and data engineering into one AI role.

AI Evaluation measures declared behavior; it does not decide professional canon. Machine Learning Engineering owns model-development machinery, not product truth. AI Systems Engineering owns model/runtime composition, not model-quality claims. Knowledge Engineering owns explicit knowledge structure/provenance, not domain truth by fiat. Data Engineering owns reliable data movement/transformation, not business semantics.

All five remain EXPERIMENTAL. Structural validity is not professional competence and does not move the 0/100 usable-profession counter.
