# Research / Accessibility / Creative Cohort

Adds five separate `EXPERIMENTAL` human professions:

- UX Researcher
- Accessibility Specialist
- Visual Designer
- Sound Designer
- Creative Director

This deliberately decomposes prior institution/studio role bundles. UX Research owns human evidence, not product decisions. Accessibility owns accessibility evidence, not legal certification. Visual Design owns visual craft, not user research. Sound Design owns sonic feedback/craft, not composition/final mix/runtime code. Creative Direction owns creative coherence, not institutional merge authority or specialist craft ownership.

ISO 9241-210:2019 is used as a bounded human-centred design seed for UX Research, and WCAG 2.2 as the primary web accessibility seed for Accessibility Specialist. No fake universal external standard is invented for Visual Design, Sound Design, or Creative Direction.

After this cohort the registry reaches 20 implemented packages; all remain `EXPERIMENTAL`, so the evidence-backed usable milestone remains `0/100`.
