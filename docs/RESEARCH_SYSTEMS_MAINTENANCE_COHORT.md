# Research, Systems & Maintenance Cohort

Adds five isolated EXPERIMENTAL Profession Packages: Technical Writer, Research Methodologist, Statistician, Systems Engineer, and Maintenance Engineer.

This completes the original 30-lane priority workfloor. Research Methodology and Statistics stay separate; Systems Engineering coordinates without becoming hierarchy; Maintenance Engineering has explicit physical-safety and authorization STOP boundaries; Technical Writing cannot turn fluent prose into technical truth.

Bounded primary seeds: ISO/IEC/IEEE 26514:2022, ASA Ethical Guidelines for Statistical Practice (2022), ISO/IEC/IEEE 15288:2023, and sector-specific ISO 14224:2016. Research Methodologist deliberately has no fake universal single-standard authority.

Structural validation does not establish professional competence. All remain EXPERIMENTAL until profession-relevant evidence supports TEST or higher.
